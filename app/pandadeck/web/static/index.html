01 — Panel

Your Panda Touch

The app finds the panel by itself over USB-C, and falls back to Wi-Fi when the cable isn't plugged in.

02 — Pages

What the panel shows

Turn pages on or off and drag them into the order you like. The tabs on the panel follow this list.

03 — Deck

Buttons and apps

The DECK page is a touch stream deck. Put your apps, files, folders and websites on it, record keyboard macros, and choose which pages of buttons it shows.

04 — Music

Now playing

The MUSIC page shows what this computer plays, with the cover or the artist's photo, a progress bar you can drag and big controls. Signing in to Spotify is optional: it adds reliable shuffle / repeat and control of Spotify on your other devices.

05 — AI optional

Mission control for coding agents

If you use Claude Code or Codex, the panel can show live sessions, your usage limits, and a big Approve / Deny when an agent asks for permission. Nothing here is on until you turn it on.

✨ Generate with AI (button pictures, in the Deck editor) also uses them: your own Claude Code or Codex sign-in on this computer and your subscription, nothing else. Those runs never show up here.

06 — HUD

Live stats

This computer's numbers show up automatically. Add a home server (Linux, macOS or Windows) if you have one.

07 — Startup

Always ready

PandaDeck runs quietly in the background. Start it with your computer so the panel is alive the moment you sit down.
